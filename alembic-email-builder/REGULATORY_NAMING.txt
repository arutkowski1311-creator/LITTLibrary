ALEMBIC PRODUCT-NAMING STANDARD
===============================

For a branded medicine, use the full legal product name once per email page,
at the first or most prominent use:

  BRAND® (generic name)

After that first legal-name use, use the brand name alone. Do not repeat the
registered mark or the generic-name pairing unless Regulatory instructs you to.
Generic names remain lower case when paired with a brand name.

The builder initially places the legal product name in a compact two-line block.
That block is optional and can be removed when an approved product logo or
another most-prominent use already provides the full legal name. It can be added
again from the Add tab.

The builder provides this treatment as an editable design option. It does not
enforce product naming, count brand/generic pairings, or block handoff based on
the presence or absence of this block.

The product-name block, Indication, Important Safety Information, and legal
footer can all be added repeatedly, duplicated, edited, moved, or removed.

For a generic-only medicine, the blank framework creates an optional compact
product-name row without adding a trademark symbol.

Preflight verifies technical email production and rendering safeguards only.
Business and legal reviewers remain responsible for the final campaign content.
