Our Story

About the Property

Tucked into a peaceful wooded setting in Wilmington, New York, our custom mountain property offers a quiet Adirondack escape just minutes from Whiteface Mountain and many of the region's best outdoor experiences.

The property includes two separate accommodations:

Ausable House

A spacious custom mountain home designed for gathering, relaxing, and enjoying everything the Adirondacks have to offer.

The Perch

A private second-floor apartment located above the garage, offering guests a cozy and independent retreat of their own.

Each accommodation can be rented separately, or Ausable House and The Perch may be rented together when availability allows. This makes the property a great option for couples, families, groups of friends, or larger parties who want to stay close while still having their own private space.

The home feels secluded enough to offer peace, privacy, and a real connection to nature, while remaining conveniently close to Whiteface Mountain, Lake Placid, hiking trails, mountain biking, swimming, skiing, and trophy trout fishing on the Ausable River.

I bought this property because the Adirondacks have always held a special place in my life. Some of my strongest childhood memories were formed here exploring the woods, spending time near the water, and developing a sense of wonder and connection to nature that has stayed with me ever since.

My hope is to give others the opportunity to experience that same feeling. Whether you come to ski, hike, ride, fish, swim, explore, or simply enjoy the quiet, this property was created to be more than just a place to stay.

It is a home base for adventure, exploration, and making your own Adirondack memories.
