Indication
PIVYA is a penicillin-class antibacterial indicated for the treatment of female patients 18 years of age and older with uncomplicated urinary tract infections (uUTI) caused by susceptible isolates of Escherichia coli, Proteus mirabilis and Staphylococcus saprophyticus. Dosing: 185 mg tablet orally three times daily for 3–7 days.

Important safety information.

Contraindications

• Serious hypersensitivity reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis or Stevens-Johnson syndrome) to PIVYA or other beta-lactam antibacterial drugs (e.g., penicillins, cephalosporins).
• Primary or secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from inherited disorders of mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation and carnitine metabolism, and other inborn errors of metabolism.
• Acute porphyria.

Warnings & Precautions

• Hypersensitivity Reactions: Serious reactions including anaphylaxis reported. Discontinue if reactions occur.
• Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions: AGEP, DRESS, SJS, TEN reported. Discontinue at first signs.
• Carnitine Depletion: Consider alternatives in at-risk patients. Not recommended for prolonged treatment. Avoid valproate.
• Acute Porphyria: Contraindicated; associated with acute attacks.
• C. difficile-Associated Diarrhea: Evaluate if diarrhea occurs.
• Drug-Resistant Bacteria: Use only for proven/suspected bacterial infections.
• Newborn Screening: May cause false positive for isovaleric acidemia.

Adverse Reactions

The most common adverse reactions (≥2%) in clinical trials were nausea and diarrhea.
