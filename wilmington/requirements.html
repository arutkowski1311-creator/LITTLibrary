The operating idea

Five decisions carry the whole build. Everything numbered after this exists to make one of them true, testable and survivable in production.

Inventory
Main house; apartment; main house + apartment together.
Distribution
Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and a direct-booking website, subject to verified channel setup.
Pricing
48-hour near-term review; monthly review through eight months; longer event protection.
Guest experience
Clear unit selection, verified amenities, signed terms, practical local guide and date-aware concierge.
Delivery
Established booking/channel manager first; a branded site and automation layer connected to it.

How to read this document

This is a build and procurement brief, not evidence of a live system. Recommendations, budgets and service targets are planning assumptions until approved. Public-source references are identified as [S##] and linked in the source notes; [P1] and [P2] identify prior owner documents. Property facts, legal terms and rates still requiring verification are called out explicitly.

Success criteria

A guest can choose the right accommodation, see an honest total price, book without conflicting inventory, receive the correct documents and arrival information, and get useful help before and during the stay.

The owner can see reservation, payment, cleaning, pricing and channel exceptions in one dashboard.

Scope boundary

The first release is not a new travel marketplace, a custom channel manager or a new revenue-management algorithm.

Local guide referrals can link to RŌM or other approved providers, but the rental must work independently. A complete legal contract, actual competitive rate study, permit approval and current property inspection are separate work items.

INV-01P0

Resource-based availability

An offer is available only when every physical resource it needs is free for every occupied night and applicable buffer. Model stays as check-in inclusive, checkout exclusive in America/New_York time.

INV-02P0

No cascading over-block

A house booking creates a derived block on HA but must not block A. A derived combined block is not a new physical reservation. The reverse rule applies to an apartment booking.

INV-03P0

Cancellation and modification

Release only the resources actually owned by the canceled reservation. Acquire the new dates before releasing the old dates during a move. Never reopen dates still consumed by another booking, owner stay, maintenance block or buffer.

INV-04P0

Concurrency and race windows

Direct checkout must revalidate H/A reservations and holds, not only the displayed HA calendar. Require a test showing that two competing direct checkouts cannot both confirm overlapping resources. If a vendor cannot demonstrate this, use request-to-book for the affected offer until a safe workflow is proven.

INV-05P0

Shared spaces and access

Each product must state whether other guests may occupy the property and which entrance, parking spaces, outdoor areas and amenities are exclusive or shared. Whole-property exclusivity may be claimed only for a genuine HA booking.

Vendor limitation to test

OwnerRez describes linked updates as near-real-time, within a minute or two. This is not proof of an atomic transaction across all online travel agencies.

Test the race window and keep an outage / overbooking response procedure. [S01]

04

Master calendar, channels and migration

Priority is accurate availability and clear recovery from failures, not simply displaying several colored calendars.

CAL-01P0

Master calendar

Show H, A and HA with channel, reservation status, guest count, owner blocks, maintenance, cleaning and payment/signature exceptions. Provide mobile day/week/month views and a property-wide closure action.

CAL-02P0

Channel connections

Use supported direct integrations for Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Map each sellable listing explicitly. Record which fields synchronize: availability, rates, minimum stays, arrival rules, fees, taxes, content and messages. Do not assume identical support. [S02]

CAL-03P0

iCal only as a fallback

iCal may serve as a secondary reference or supported fallback, not the primary protection for simultaneous bookings. Airbnb states external calendar imports refresh automatically every three hours. [S03]

CAL-04P0

Synchronization health

Track last successful import/export, failed updates and unresolved acknowledgments. Retry safely, deduplicate repeated messages and reconcile full future reservation/availability snapshots. Escalate conflicts rather than silently overwriting them.

CAL-05P0

Outage response

Freeze risky instant-booking products or shorten available inventory exposure when synchronization is unreliable, to the extent the vendor/channel allows. Notify the owner and local backup; document guest relocation/refund escalation without claiming zero cross-channel risk.

CAL-06P0

Owner control

Support date ranges, recurring blocks, reasons, expiry and user audit history. Maintenance staff cannot override a guest reservation. Manual rate/calendar overrides show their owner and end date.

Migration runbook

Export future reservations, cancellations, holds and rates before connecting channels. Reconcile existing manual cross-blocks and preserve their reasons in a backup. During a controlled sales pause, remove only obsolete imitation blocks before enabling logical links; retain genuine owner and maintenance closures. Then verify every future stay. OwnerRez warns that residual manual cross-blocks can cascade unexpectedly. [S01]

Preserve existing listing IDs, reviews and authorized account ownership wherever channel rules allow. Run a short controlled change window, compare website and channel calendars, test vendor-supported booking scenarios and monitor the first live reservations. Do not delete proven listings merely to appear new.

Proposed operating targets: immediate owner visibility of a new core reservation; alerts within five minutes of detecting a failed critical sync; daily reconciliation. Final achievable timings must be confirmed with the vendor and each channel.

05

Direct booking and agreement requirements

This section is an instruction set for the application and a New York attorney. It is not a ready-to-sign liability waiver.

BOOK-01P0

Transparent shopping

Search dates and party size; compare three offers without mixing their amenities. Show the total required stay price, itemized mandatory fees, tax, refund terms, security arrangement and optional extras before commitment. Preserve the accepted quote. [S12]

BOOK-02P0

Reservation lifecycle

Support quote, short-lived hold, pending payment/signature, confirmed, in-house, departed and closed states, with cancellation/refund branches. A guest must understand when a reservation is pending versus confirmed. Expired holds release safely.

BOOK-03P0

Controlled confirmation

Use a documented order for payment and signatures. Do not leave a charged guest with an untracked failed reservation. Failed steps trigger retry, staff review or a clear cancellation/refund outcome. Bookings through other channels follow their authorized workflow.

LEG-01P0

Core rental agreement

Attorney-approved terms must identify the actual contracting entity, specific accommodation, approved occupants, dates, price, payment schedule, cancellation, refunds, visitors, pets, smoking, parties, quiet hours, access, emergencies and applicable dispute provisions.

LEG-02P0

Separate schedules and disclosures

Attach the house rules, amenity-use instructions, shared-space disclosure, damage/payment authorization, cancellation summary and privacy notice. Apply only the schedules relevant to the booked unit and available equipment.

LEG-03P0

Evidence of agreement

Retain the exact version presented, accepted quote, affirmative acceptance, signer identity, date/time and audit record. Provide a downloadable signed copy. Revised terms do not silently alter an existing reservation.

LEG-04P0

Liability language

Counsel must review releases, indemnities and adult/guardian signatures. Do not assume one primary guest binds every adult or can waive every claim of a minor. New York statutes limit certain negligence waivers; applicability depends on the arrangement. [S08, S09]

Required changes to the earlier draft

Reconcile the legal entity; establish per-product occupancy; replace the months-long hold assumption; narrow overbroad releases; review damage assessments, entry, cancellation and refund language; remove irrelevant or unavailable amenities; and verify any role assigned to RŌM.

The prior draft's "For attorney review" label must not be treated as an approval. [P1]

06

Payments, compliance and practical protection

Contracts work alongside insurance, compliant operations and documented maintenance. They do not make the property immune to liability.

PAY-01P0

Secure payment handling

Use the booking platform's supported tokenized/hosted processor. Do not store full card numbers. Specify deposit/balance timing, accepted currencies, off-session charge consent, failed-payment handling, refunds and chargeback evidence.

PAY-02P0

Damage security

Use a disclosed, legally approved deposit, near-arrival authorization or eligible protection product. Schedule authorization around arrival and inspect promptly after departure. Ordinary online card authorizations are commonly valid for about seven days; extended windows are conditional, not a solution for a booking months away. [S10, S11]

COM-01P0

Wilmington approval

Verify the current Town of Wilmington license, county registration and permitted rental configuration before advertising or renting. Confirm H, A and HA, occupancy, sleeping areas, parking, septic capacity and any applicable renewal/inspection requirements. Do not substitute Lake Placid or North Elba rules. [S06]

COM-02P0

Tax responsibility matrix

A qualified tax adviser must specify taxes, taxable fees, registrations and remittance responsibility for each channel and direct bookings. New York's short-term-rental sales-tax provisions took effect 1 March 2025. A channel's collection does not establish collection for the direct site. [S07, S37]

COM-03P0

Insurance approval

Have an STR insurance broker confirm direct and channel bookings, separate and simultaneous unit rentals, all advertised amenities, liability, property damage, interruption and relevant exclusions. Standard homeowners coverage may not cover this exposure. [S15]

COM-04P0

Channel-specific rules

Apply channel-approved payment, deposit, agreement and communications workflows. Do not divert an Airbnb inquiry off-platform or request guest details there for unauthorized marketing. Guest service communications and promotional enrollment are distinct. [S13]

SAFE-01P0

Safety operating record

Maintain inspection and service logs for alarms, exits, extinguishers, electrical/heating systems, stairs, snow/ice and any recreational amenities. Maintain a local emergency contact, incident procedure and property-specific posted instructions.

Launch evidence

Attorney approval of current documents; insurance confirmation naming the right entities and exposures; valid property/unit approvals; written tax matrix; tested payment/refund flow; named local responder.

No document here confirms that these approvals currently exist.

07

Dynamic pricing: inputs and review cadence

Optimize contribution from the property, not just occupancy or a higher advertised nightly rate. House, apartment and combined offer require separate positioning.

Urgent exception — event change, outage, material demand signal or owner request: recheck immediately; log the reason and protect existing confirmed prices.

REV-01P0

Comp sets

Start with approximately 10–20 reviewed comparables for each product where enough genuine matches exist. Match sleeping layout, party size, drive location, privacy, quality and verified amenities. Do not use a large premium house as the apartment's price anchor.

REV-02P0

Comparable total cost

Compare equivalent dates, length of stay, guest count, mandatory fees, taxes where applicable, cancellation terms and minimum stays. Distinguish advertised asking prices, modeled market estimates and the property's actual realized revenue.

REV-03P0

Demand signals

Combine licensed market data with this property's booking lead time, pickup, cancellations, sellable nights and conversion. An unavailable competitor night is not proof of a paid booking. No unauthorized scraping or invented competitor occupancy.

REV-04P0

Event and holiday registry

Store source, location, date, confirmed/tentative status, likely audience, travel window and next verification. Add federal holidays and source-market school calendars only from current official calendars. Do not assume all districts share breaks.

REV-05P0

Cadence preference

A provider's daily rate updates may be enabled with owner approval; otherwise match the intended 48-hour publishing rule. Either way, issue a concise 48-hour owner digest. Explain material changes rather than sending every nightly price.

The long-range event safeguard is an addition to, not a replacement for, the requested 90-day and six-to-eight-month reviews. Actual starting rates require current comparable quotes and verified property costs.

08

Pricing guardrails and combined-offer economics

One authorized rate writer, explicit floors, and a bundle price that is chosen rather than assumed. A combined booking occupies two units, not three.

REV-06P0

Floors and ceilings

Set a per-product minimum contribution and seasonal rate bounds. Include cleaning shortfall, laundry, utilities, consumables, channel/processor costs, discounts and other incremental costs. Treat tax collected for government separately from revenue.

REV-07P0

One rate writer

Disable competing native auto-pricing where it would conflict. The approved pricing service sends rates to the booking core, which distributes them. Manual overrides have explicit precedence, reason, expiry and a record of the prior value.

REV-08P0

Change control

As an initial owner-review rule, flag changes above 15% from the last approved price, below-floor quotes, unexpected event discounts and sharp restriction changes. This threshold is a proposed safeguard, not an industry benchmark.

REV-09P0

Bundle price

Choose a supported method: an independently reviewed HA price, or an explicit calculation from H and A with a controlled discount/premium. Confirm the integration can execute the method. PriceLabs' combined-listing mapping affects analytics and is not, by itself, a guaranteed H+A pricing formula. [S04]

REV-10P0

Opportunity cost

Assess an HA booking against the expected net value of separate H and A bookings, not automatically against their simple sum. Protect peak dates from unnecessary bundle discounts; permit off-peak flexibility when it adds contribution.

REV-11P0

Restriction management

Support day-of-week rules, minimum/maximum stays, gap-night strategies, arrival lead time and turnover buffers. Do not use a stay restriction that makes a profitable small gap permanently impossible to book.

REV-12P0

Failure fallback

Retain the last approved valid rates if market data or synchronization fails. Never publish zero, an empty price or a default low rate because a feed is unavailable. Reconcile what each channel actually displays.

Measurement definitions

Track paid occupied physical-unit nights ÷ sellable physical-unit nights. A combined booking occupies two units, not three.

Keep owner use, maintenance closures and unsold nights distinguishable. Allocate HA revenue to physical units under a consistent reporting policy without duplicating income. Track actual accommodation revenue per occupied unit-night, net contribution, booking lead time, cancellation-adjusted pickup and channel acquisition cost. Channel commission structures must come from the live account; do not assume every Airbnb host pays the same percentage. [S14]

WEB-01P0

Essential pages

Home, main house, apartment, entire property, availability, amenities, gallery/video, area guide, seasonal guides, offers, practical FAQs, contact, booking policies, privacy and accessibility information.

WEB-02P0

Unit-specific presentation

Publish verified beds, bathrooms, occupancy, layout, stairs, kitchen facilities, climate control, Wi-Fi, work space, parking and accessibility details. Confirm whether the apartment is an enclosed one-bedroom or an open studio before naming it.

WEB-03P0

Gallery and video

WEB-04P0

State shared spaces, possible neighboring guests, noise/road conditions, winter access, seasonal limitations and unavailable equipment before checkout. Guests should not discover limitations in the arrival email.

10

Public guide and personalized concierge

A useful guide is a maintained information service, not a one-time collection of links. P0 can use verified templates and manual personalization; automated itinerary assembly and refresh are P1.

GUE-01P0

Always-available guide

Publish dining, groceries, emergency help, skiing, hiking, biking, sightseeing, children's options, rainy-day activities and local guides/outfitters. Add distance/drive context, seasonal limits and official links after verification.

GUE-02P0

Source registry

Each recommendation stores its source, last verification, season/hours, location, cost category, booking requirements, accessibility facts and suitability. Use official sources first; mark stale or unverified information rather than inventing specifics.

GUE-03P0

Optional personalization

Invite travel purpose, interests, budget, children's age bands, preferred pace and voluntarily provided dietary/access needs. Keep the form optional and proportionate. Guests who skip it receive a useful default itinerary.

GUE-04P0

Deliverables

Provide a mobile-friendly private itinerary and printable/offline summary. Separate personal and access information from the public, indexable guide. Access codes must never appear in public pages or broadly shared PDFs.

11

Operations, permissions and reliability

The booking system must create the work required to deliver the stay, not just collect money.

OPS-01P0

Turnover and maintenance

Create unit-specific cleaning tasks, arrival/checkout times, linen/supply needs and completion checklists. A combined stay generates the correct work for both units. Confirm same-day turnovers only when staffing and required service intervals allow.

OPS-02P0

Access control

Issue time-limited access for the booked unit(s), test combined bookings and retain a local fallback. Never give an apartment guest a house code. Revoke canceled/expired access; protect owner and service credentials.

OPS-03P0

Unified work queue

Show today's arrivals/departures, unfinished cleans, guest issues, missing signatures, failed payments, rate exceptions, license renewals and channel failures. Assign an owner and deadline to each exception.

OPS-04P0

Human escalation

Name a local responder and backup for lockouts, snow/ice, power, plumbing and safety issues. Automated replies must not imply emergency services have been contacted or a repair is arranged unless confirmed.

DATA-01P0

Core records

Maintain physical units, sellable offers, listing mappings, reservations/holds, quotes, rate plans, payments/refunds, agreement versions, guest consents, amenities, guide entries, events, tasks and audit events. Use persistent external IDs for imports and deduplication.

DATA-02P0

Permissions and privacy

Owner/admin controls finances and inventory; cleaners see only operational details; editors cannot publish prices or access codes. Require multi-factor authentication for privileged users, encrypted connections, secret management and minimum necessary personal data.

DATA-03P0

Retention and recovery

Agree retention periods with counsel and the processor. Export reservations, finances, signed documents and content. Keep backups, test restoration, and document how to operate during a vendor outage or change providers.

NFR-01P0

Quality targets

Set mobile accessibility and performance acceptance targets; test keyboard navigation, labels, contrast and captions. Suggested performance goals: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms and CLS ≤ 0.1 on the main public templates, subject to real-device measurement.

Limits on automated assistants

Automated assistants may draft content and answer from verified property information.

They may not autonomously promise refunds, legal protections, special access, safe outdoor conditions or unapproved rate exceptions. Log drafts and approvals.

12

SEO, referrals and marketing connections

The detailed promotion calendar and budget are in the companion launch plan. These requirements make that plan executable.

SEO-01P0

Indexable content

Serve useful, fast, crawlable product and guide pages with unique titles, headings, descriptions, internal links, canonical URLs and sitemap. Keep private guest pages, checkout and duplicate search/filter pages out of indexing. Preserve valuable old URLs with appropriate redirects. [S17, S36]

SEO-02P0

Links and local discovery

Link to relevant attractions for guest usefulness, not as a claimed automatic ranking boost. Seek legitimate local listings and partnerships. Qualify paid/affiliate links appropriately. [S17, S20]

SEO-03P0

Google eligibility

Evaluate a supported Google Vacation Rentals connection and accurate structured data. Markup does not guarantee acceptance. Do not create an ordinary Google Business Profile for an ineligible individual vacation home. [S18, S19]

MKT-01P1

Content queue

Store campaign, target stay dates, product, audience, source links, offer, media rights, landing page, tracking code, approval, publication time and expiry. Generate drafts from verified seasonal/event content; require owner approval initially.

MKT-02P1

Availability-aware promotion

Suppress or replace ads and offers when their advertised dates sell, an amenity closes or an event changes. Refresh factual claims at publication, not only when a draft is written.

LOY-01P1

Repeat and referral rules

Allow unique codes, eligible date/unit/channel rules, expiry, minimum spend/stay, redemption limits and non-stacking rules. Award referral credit only after a completed qualifying stay. Refunds reverse unearned credits.

LOY-02P1

Consent and fairness

Marketing opt-in is separate from booking acceptance. Maintain suppression/unsubscribe lists; do not condition incentives on a positive review. Do not reuse channel guest details for marketing contrary to channel restrictions. [S13]

ANA-01P0

Reliable attribution

Track campaign clicks, date searches, quote/checkout starts and confirmed paid bookings with consistent IDs. Reconcile to actual refunds and cancellations. Avoid duplicate conversions across a hosted checkout and the website.

Owner dashboard

Sellable / occupied unit-nights, accommodation revenue, net contribution, direct share, booking lead time, paid acquisition cost, cancellations, repeat/referral bookings, unfinished work and data freshness.

Do not report three units of capacity or three units of income from an HA reservation.
